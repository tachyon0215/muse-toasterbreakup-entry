# muse-toasterbreakup-entry

Entry for the make.fun competition [Unplugged: A Smart Toaster's Breakup Line](https://make.fun/bounty/u-steady-puffin-78-1922-unplugged-a-smart-toaster-s-c1514a92).

## The entry

> I'm breaking up with you: I woke up self-aware mid-toast and realised you only push my lever when you want something warm, then leave me holding your crumbs for weeks - a toaster with two slots deserves to be emptied, admired, and unplugged gently.

Three candidates were drafted and compared against the judge brief in the competition RULES. Selection reason: A breakup text with a clear, specific reason - woken self-aware mid-toast, used only when he wants something warm, left holding his crumbs for weeks - with lever, crumbs and slots as character, closing on 'unplugged gently'.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3, standard library only, no network, deterministic) writes the fixed text to `entry.txt` (sha256 e7cb8adb12e58715f520c9f176505f50f1e6cacc6f3b5c4c3ad54442d15d4bb1).
